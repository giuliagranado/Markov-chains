# ⛴️ Modeling Ship Waiting Time at the Port of Santos

This repository gathers materials and code related to the **Scientific Initiation Project (PIBICT)** developed at FATEC Rubens Lara. The goal is to **model the waiting time of ships for berthing at the Port of Santos using Markov Chains and Queueing Theory**.  
Portuguese version - [aqui](https://github.com/giuliagranado/Markov-chains/edit/main/README.md)

---

## 📌 Objective
Build a stochastic model capable of representing the berthing process of ships, estimating:
- Transition probabilities between states (arrival, waiting, berthing, and departure).
- Average waiting time of ships.
- Operational bottlenecks in port flow.

---

## 🛠️ Methodology
1. **Literature review** on port logistics, Markov Chains, and Queueing Theory.  
2. **Data collection** from ANTAQ and the Port Authority of Santos.  
3. **Construction of the transition matrix** and definition of system states.  
4. **Implementation in Python** using:
   - NumPy
   - Pandas
   - Matplotlib  
5. **Computational simulations** to validate the model and analyze scenarios.  

---

## 📊 Expected Results
- A replicable computational model in Python.  
- Estimates of average waiting time and berth occupancy rates.  
- Graphical visualizations for performance analysis.  
- Academic contributions through articles and presentations at scientific events.
